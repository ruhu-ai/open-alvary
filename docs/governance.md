# Governance

Open Alvary is a shared public legal-data layer. Alvary's proprietary application
is a downstream consumer with no special access to public corpus records. Customer
and commercially licensed material is not contributed here. Hosted compute may
later have paid tiers without changing the data's recorded reuse rights.

Proposed roles: maintainers own software/release integrity; qualified rights
reviewers record permissions and conditions; content reviewers verify text,
structure, currentness and source anchors. These are proposed operating roles,
not a claim that a governing body or review team has been appointed.

Rights and legal-content verification are independent. Keep privileged advice and
private personal details outside public records. Public records should contain
an appropriate reviewer identifier and a publishable evidence summary.

Before launch, name accountable owners and publish a working corrections/takedown
channel. A credible rights or privacy complaint should trigger a publication hold,
review of affected hashes, suspension of old releases, and a documented resolution.
Database updates to rights invalidate affected release delivery, but external copies
cannot be recalled. External mirrors need an explicit withdrawal protocol.

A software licence is included; catalogue licensing, contribution terms, conflict
handling and a sustainable funding model remain governance decisions.
