# Rights policy v0.1

This is an engineering publication policy, not a legal opinion about Nigerian law.
No public-domain assumption is made. Official URLs establish origin, not permission.

| Status | Metadata | Full text |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN | Only with recorded metadata basis | Only after all gates pass |
| OPEN_WITH_CONDITIONS | Only with recorded metadata basis | All gates plus documented, satisfied conditions |
| LINK_ONLY | Only with recorded metadata basis | Never |
| RIGHTS_UNDER_REVIEW | Only with recorded metadata basis | Never |
| RESTRICTED / missing record | Never | Never |

Full-text gates require exact approved normalized hashes; affirmative commercial
reuse and derivatives permissions; redistribution yes or conditional; holder,
licence, dated reviewer, evidence links, legal note and database-rights note;
privacy cleared/redacted; known attribution requirement with supplied attribution
where needed; recorded conditions satisfied; and independently verified content.
Expired/future-dated reviews fail closed. Translations require approval of their
own hashes and traceable original and translated versions.

`conditions_satisfied` is a reviewer assertion about supported release conditions,
not automated legal reasoning. Do not approve conditions that JSONL/API distribution
cannot satisfy. A noncommercial or no-derivatives source is not suitable for this
shared open full-text layer. A metadata-only entry may still link to it where lawful.

All seed legal-text rights remain unknown. Metadata is independently authored;
its recorded publication basis is separate from legal-text clearance. Reviewer,
review date, content retrieval date and hashes stay unset when no corresponding
review or ingestion happened. Never fabricate those values to make validation pass.

API and exports share the same gate. Exact-hash approval cannot carry over to a
new version. Download checks suspend stale or withdrawn releases. The software
cannot itself establish legal rights or enforce all downstream licence obligations.
